Why I'm Running

I am running to strengthen the office of Area D Director and help Roberts Creek move from reactive government toward deliberate, accountable government.

Six Priorities

Protect Our Watershed & Aquifer

Establish what we are protecting, build the monitoring needed to understand its condition, and pursue the appropriate protections.

Renew the OCP Without Losing Roberts Creek

Modernize our planning framework while preserving meaningful local participation and the character of Roberts Creek.

Turn the Water Strategy Into Water in the Pipes

Move from studying water supply to delivering dependable new sources with clear milestones, costs and public reporting.

Plan the Future of Fire Protection

Build on existing planning with a clear long-term service, staffing, equipment and capital plan residents can understand.

Make Decisions on Waste & Recycling

Reduce what goes to landfill and bring unresolved questions such as curbside recycling to an evidence-based conclusion.

Steward Our Parks & Community Lands

Plan their future deliberately, understand their legal protections and involve the community before major changes are effectively committed.
