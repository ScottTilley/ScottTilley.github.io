Six Priorities

Practical goals that move Area D from one-off reactions toward deliberate, accountable government.

1

Protect Our Watershed & Aquifer

Roberts Creek needs an explicit watershed-protection objective. We should work with First Nations, the Province, water users, the scientific community and conservation groups to establish what we are protecting, build the monitoring necessary to understand its condition, and then pursue the appropriate tools under the Water Sustainability Act. That should include examining legally enforceable Water Objectives and, where the evidence and risks warrant it, a Water Sustainability Plan.

2

Renew the OCP Without Losing Roberts Creek

A renewed OCP should preserve what makes Roberts Creek Roberts Creek while bringing our planning framework up to date. Area D’s existing OCP should be renewed through meaningful community participation, updated for current conditions and provincial requirements, and retain strong local involvement through bodies such as the RCOCPC. Regional integration should improve planning, not weaken local accountability.

3

Turn the Water Strategy Into Water in the Pipes

Move from studying water supply to delivering dependable new water sources. The SCRD appears to have converged on a rational supply strategy; the goal now should be clear milestones, urgency in execution, transparent costs, and regular public reporting until new supply is actually connected to the system.

4

Plan the Future of Fire Protection

Establish a clear, updated long-term plan for the fire service Roberts Creek needs and can sustain. Define the service model, staffing, equipment, facilities and replacement needs, forecast their costs, and explain clearly to residents why those investments are required. Major budget changes should emerge from a plan, not arrive as surprises.

5

Make Decisions on Waste & Recycling

Reduce what Roberts Creek sends to landfill while bringing unresolved service questions to a conclusion. Protect the community that hosts the landfill, continue improving diversion, and make a clear evidence-based decision on curbside recycling using current costs, environmental benefits and accessibility—not another cycle of study and deferral.

6

Steward Our Parks & Community Lands

Protect Roberts Creek’s parks and community lands through deliberate long-term stewardship. Understand the legal protections, intended uses and future needs of these properties; engage the community before significant changes are effectively committed; and adopt clear park plans so individual proposals are considered within an agreed vision rather than driving the vision themselves.
